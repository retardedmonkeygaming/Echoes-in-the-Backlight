═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
ERIN's real name is Elena Voss. She was 23 years old.
She was a quiet, kind painter who lived alone in a small apartment.
She had no friends, no family. She loved solitude.
She met Marcus Hale in a small bookstore on the edge of the city.
He was charming, tall, and intensely quiet.
They talked for hours about nothing and everything.
One night she invited him to her apartment because "the city is too loud."
That was the last night she was ever seen alive.
